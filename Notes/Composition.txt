Composition -> means one class is built out of other, and those objects are usually created right inside __init__ and stored 
as attributes.

In composition, one of the classes is composed of one or more instance of other classes. In other words, 
one class is container and other class is content and if you delete the container object then all of its contents objects are also 
deleted.


*NOTE - remember the phrase -> ("Part-Of")

* It's a design princible where compelx systems or objects are built by combining similar, simpler, reusable peices of code. 
* Examples: Car (object) and engine is ("Part-Of") a car. This goes for wheels, and so on.
